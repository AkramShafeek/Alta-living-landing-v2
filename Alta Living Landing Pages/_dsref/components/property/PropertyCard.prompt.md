Use for any home in a grid or carousel. Three per row on desktop.

```jsx
<PropertyCard src="assets/homes/1.jpg" title="The Garden Loft"
  hook="A quiet 2BHK above a courtyard, five minutes from 100ft Road."
  location="Indiranagar, Bangalore" beds="2BHK" availability="Available 1/3"
  price="₹ 3,200" unit="per night" />
```

Cool `--wash` ground behind a warm photo is intentional. Keep square corners and the 1px ink border.
