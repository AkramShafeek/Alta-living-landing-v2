## 7. FAQ Section

**[UI: accordion list, 6 items, collapsed by default]**

**Section heading:**
> Questions, answered

**Q: How is Alta Living different from Airbnb?**
A: We personally manage and vet every home on our platform — we're not an open marketplace. That means consistent quality, real photos, and a local contact for every stay.

**Q: What's included in the price?**
A: Utilities, Wi-Fi, and cleaning before check-in are always included. The price you see at checkout is the price you pay — no surprise fees.

**Q: Can I book for a single night, or only longer stays?**
A: Most homes are available from a single night, though many guests choose to stay a week or more — our homes are built for both.

**Q: Is there a security deposit?**
A: Yes, a refundable deposit is collected at booking and released within 48 hours of checkout, assuming no damage.

**Q: What if something goes wrong during my stay?**
A: Every guest gets a direct number for a local Alta Living contact — not a support ticket queue. Most issues are resolved same-day.

**Q: Which cities are you in right now?**
A: Bangalore, Goa, and Coorg today, with new cities being added through the year. Join our list to hear when we launch near you.

## Notes on tone/voice for whoever writes final copy
- Voice is **warm, specific, slightly editorial** — not corporate travel-brand voice, not budget-Airbnb voice.
- Avoid generic hospitality clichés ("unforgettable experience," "home away from home") — lean on concrete, sensory detail instead (the coffee estate, the courtyard, the plunge pool).
- All numbers/testimonials/names above are placeholders written to *sound* real and specific — swap in actual stats, reviews, and property names before shipping.
